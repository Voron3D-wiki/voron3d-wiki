# Printers for Ants

"Printers for Ants" covers the small-format corner of the Voron ecosystem —
designs with build volumes below roughly 180 mm. The Micron is the best known of
these: essentially a scaled-down Voron 2.4 with a flying gantry and 1515
extrusions.
