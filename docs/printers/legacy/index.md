# Legacy Voron Printers

These are Voron designs that have been superseded by the current lineup. They
are documented here because plenty of them are still running, still being bought
second-hand, and still being upgraded — but none of them are the right starting
point for a new build. For a new printer see the
[current printers overview](../index.md).

## Models

| Model | Notes |
|:------|:------|
| [Voron V1.6](v1-6/index.md) | Cartesian frame; the last of the V1 line before the Trident replaced it |
| [Voron V1.8](v1-8/index.md) | Incremental V1 revision, largely superseded by the Trident |

## What replaced them

- The [Voron Trident](../trident/index.md) is the modern successor to the V1 line.
- The [Voron V2.4](../2.4/index.md) is the flying-gantry CoreXY.
- The [Voron Switchwire](../switchwire/index.md) covers the small cartesian niche.

!!! warning "Sourcing parts"
    Legacy models use extrusion sizes, rail lengths, and printed parts that
    current kits do not ship. Check the BOM for the specific revision before
    buying anything.
