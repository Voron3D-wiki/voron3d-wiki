## Affiliate Links

Many pages on this wiki contain affiliate links to retailers such as West3D,
OneTwo3D, AliExpress, Amazon, and BIQU. If you click one of these links and make
a purchase, we may earn a small commission **at no extra cost to you**.

These commissions cover hosting and let us keep the wiki free. They do not
influence which parts we recommend — a product is listed because it is relevant
to Voron builds, not because it pays a commission.

Pages containing affiliate links carry a disclosure notice at the bottom.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. IP addresses are
anonymised and Google Signals is disabled. We do not sell or share visitor data.

## Content Accuracy

This wiki is community-maintained and provided as-is. Printer components,
firmware, and vendor listings change frequently, so always cross-check against
the [official Voron documentation](https://docs.vorondesign.com/) before buying
parts or changing a configuration.

Every page shows a "last updated" date at the bottom. If something looks out of
date, corrections are welcome via
[GitHub](https://github.com/Voron3D-wiki/voron3d-wiki) or the
[Voron Discord](https://discord.gg/voron).

## Safety

Guides on this wiki cover mains-voltage wiring, heaters, and lithium batteries.
Follow the [electronics safety guide](/electronics/safety/) and, if you
are unsure, ask before you power anything on. You are responsible for your own
build.
