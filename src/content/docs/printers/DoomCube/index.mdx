DoomCube is a community of designers and builders working on small CoreXY
printers, most of them derived from the [Voron V0](/printers/v0/). It is the
home of Zruncho's designs and the surrounding ecosystem of V0 mods.

These are not official Voron designs. They are community derivatives, and they
assume you are comfortable sourcing parts yourself and reading a repo rather
than a polished manual. If this is your first build, start with a
[stock Voron](/printers/) instead.

## Designs

| Design | Base | What it changes |
|:-------|:-----|:----------------|
| [Tri-Zero](/printers/DoomCube/TriZero/) | Voron V0 / V0.2 | Triple belted Z with automatic bed leveling; optional Plus50 for a 170 × 170 mm bed |
| [Dueling Zero](/printers/DoomCube/duelingZero/) | Voron V0 / V0.2 | Two independent gantries in one V0 frame |
