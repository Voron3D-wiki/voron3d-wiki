These are Voron designs that have been superseded by the current lineup. They
are documented here because plenty of them are still running, still being bought
second-hand, and still being upgraded — but none of them are the right starting
point for a new build. For a new printer see the
[current printers overview](/printers/).

## Models

| Model | Notes |
|:------|:------|
| [Voron V1.6](/printers/legacy/v1-6/) | Cartesian frame; the last of the V1 line before the Trident replaced it |
| [Voron V1.8](/printers/legacy/v1-8/) | Incremental V1 revision, largely superseded by the Trident |

## What replaced them

- The [Voron Trident](/printers/trident/) is the modern successor to the V1 line.
- The [Voron V2.4](/printers/2.4/) is the flying-gantry CoreXY.
- The [Voron Switchwire](/printers/switchwire/) covers the small cartesian niche.

:::caution[Sourcing parts]
Legacy models use extrusion sizes, rail lengths, and printed parts that
current kits do not ship. Check the BOM for the specific revision before
buying anything.
:::
