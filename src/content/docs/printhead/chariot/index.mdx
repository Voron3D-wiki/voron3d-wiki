## Overview

Chariot is an X carriage from VORON Design for the **Voron Trident** and **Voron V2**
printers. The X carriage rides on the X-axis rail and carries the toolhead, the probe and
the ends of the X/Y belts. Chariot replaces the stock carriage and is designed around
modern probes, especially eddy current ones.

For a comparison with the other kinds of carriage (stock, Tap, toolchanger shuttles, CNC)
and help choosing between them, see [X Carriages](/printhead/#x-carriages) on the
Printhead page.

## Why the X carriage matters

The carriage decides three things that are hard to change later:

- **Which probes you can mount.** Eddy current probes need to sit close to the nozzle and
  a fixed distance above it. On the stock carriage they need a printed adapter.
- **How the belts are held and tensioned.** The belt clamps take the full belt tension,
  and they set how easy it is to adjust that tension.
- **Which toolheads fit.** Toolheads attach through a mounting pattern, so the carriage
  limits your choice of toolhead.

## What Chariot offers

### Probe support

Chariot has a native mount for eddy current probes in the **Beacon / Cartographer size
format**. It also supports:

- [BTT Eddy](/bedleveling/eddy/)
- Klicky
- Prusa PINDA

The BTT Eddy and PINDA mounts need two extra brass heat inserts (16 rather than 14) and two
M3×20mm BHCS. The [Bed Leveling](/bedleveling/) page explains how to choose a probe.

### Belt clips

Chariot has new belt clips designed to be compact, easy to assemble and install, and easy
to adjust.

### Toolhead compatibility

Chariot supports any toolhead that uses the **Clockwork2 mounting pattern**, including:

- [Stealthburner](/printhead/stealthburner/)
- [DownDraft](/printhead/downdraft/)
- XOL
- A4T

## Hardware

The hardware list is short. It needs brass heat inserts, M3 screws (BHCS and SHCS from 6mm
to 30mm), two Makerbeam XL T-slot nuts, two M3 washers, one self-tapping M2×10mm screw and a
zip tie. The full list, with the probe-specific changes, is in the
[official BOM](https://github.com/VoronDesign/voron-chariot/blob/main/BOM.md).
