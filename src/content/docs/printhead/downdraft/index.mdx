## Overview

DownDraft is a modular toolhead from VORON Design. Its designer also created the
Dragonburner and YAVOTH toolheads. DownDraft is made for the **Voron Trident, V2, Legacy and
Switchwire**. Its aim is to let you pick your hotend, extruder and part cooling without
changing the core toolhead.

## DownDraft or Stealthburner?

| | [Stealthburner](/printhead/stealthburner/) | DownDraft |
|--|--|--|
| Extruder | Clockwork2 (built in) | Bring your own: Orbiter v2 pattern, others with a spacer |
| Hotends | Common hotends through official mounts (e.g. Dragon, Rapido, Revo Voron) | 15+ hotends, from Revo Voron to Goliath |
| Part cooling | Single 5015 | Two 4010 or two 5015 blowers |
| Toolhead boards | SB-style boards | 36mm boards, SB-style 2-part boards, PUG |
| Best for | First builds following the official manual | Builders who already know what hardware they want |

Both use the Clockwork2 carriage mounting pattern, so both fit the stock X carriage and
[Chariot](/printhead/chariot/).

## Features

- **Modular design.** Swap mounts to suit your hotend, extruder and cooling, without
  redesigning the toolhead.
- **Bring your own extruder.** Orbiter v2 mount pattern natively. LGX Lite and Sherpa Mini
  patterns fit with the alternative extruder spacer.
- **2510 hotend fan in three positions.** High, middle and low front mounts line up the
  airflow with heatsinks of different heights.
- **4010 or 5015 part cooling.** Two variants, each using two blower fans.
- **LEDs.** Neopixels or "Rainbow barf" at the logo. Nozzle lighting by Neopixel or a
  piece of 144 LED/m strip.
- **Universal mounts** for cable chains and toolhead boards, including two-part
  Stealthburner-style boards. Works with umbilical or cable-chain wiring, and supports PUG
  out of the box.
- **Easier servicing.** Better access to common maintenance areas, so less disassembly
  is needed for inspection and repairs.

## Supported hotends

The hotend sets which front mount (and so which hotend fan height) you use.

| Fan position | Hotends |
|---|---|
| **High** | Goliath / CHC XL, Dragon UHF, Rapido UHF, Rapido X, Tricorn Air Long, Dragon Ace + MZE |
| **Middle** | Dragon Ace, Dragon Volcano, Rapido HF, Tricorn Air Short, Revo Voron Volcano |
| **Low** | Dragon SF/HF, Chube Compact, TZ-V6 v2 (with V6 nozzle), Revo Voron |

For help choosing a hotend, see the [Hotends](/printhead/hotends/) page.

## Fans

DownDraft's hardware compatibility list gives these tested fans. Choose the highest
RPM version available, especially for GDStime fans, which come in several speeds.

| Role | Size | Tested fans | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotend | 2510 axial | GDStime, Vindr, Delta | |
| Part cooling | 5015 blower | GDStime (8500 rpm), Delta BFB0524HH, R3:men, Vindr Berserker, Mechatronics | 6K+ rpm recommended. The Vindr Berserker skips the spring mount and needs its front cable tabs removed. The Mechatronics fan fits very tightly. |
| Part cooling | 4010 blower | GDStime, Vindr, Honeybadger, Delta | 12K rpm recommended |

The [Fans](/electronics/fans/) page covers fan sizes and buying options.
